3. What part of the experience did you spend the most time engaging with?

I spent most of my time on the home page, where each choice was presented as a 3D element that looked like a room and the animation that kept me engaged and excited to explore it further.

4. What was the most common action in your two minute interaction with the experience?

The most common action was scrolling back up after accidentally scrolling too far. Luni website uses a lot of animation triggered by scroll, so there is a delay between my scroll and the page response. Additionally, the scroll is very sensitive to mouse, a small movement caused a large scroll, so I often skipped past content I wanted to see and had to scroll back.

5. What is your impression of the intended primary goal of the interactive experience?

From my perspective, Lumi website is a website that try to sind new people to join company by showing that they are a company that has creativity and modernity. And show their team vision, how they work for the company, and the company’s attitude. The website also clearly states that they really value freedom of creativity and teamwork. They also show welfare benefit from (flexibility – you can work remotely up to 2 day a week, learning – free language classes, master classes , Healthcare –100 percents coverage for you and your children, Wellness – full access to sport and activities, Food – meal voucher (cover 50 percent by Luni), etc)

6. How does the interactive experience communicate this primary goal?

In my perspective, the primary goal of the Luni website is to attract potential employees. In the website, they use 3D rooms instead of plain menu links which show how they focus on creativity, this might also show that this is a fun company instead of boring cooperate work. They also mention about teamwork, company vision, well-being and benefits of employees which prove that they are trying to be appealing to job hunters.
